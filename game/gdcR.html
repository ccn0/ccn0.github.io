game.cars REMASTERED is a heavily remastered edition of game.cars.

game.cars is a game where you control a blue dot and must defeat a red dot.

This remaster fixes many original issues with the game, and has many new textures, abilities, quality of life improvements, and settings.

Your goal is to get RED's health down to zero, while trying to survive as the game gets more crowded as you play.

You can both pass through your own color lines, build to stop each other's bullets, and collect and destroy power-ups.

If your game lags, try turning off HQ PEN on the title screen.
